An entry for the make.fun competition "One Line of Earned Pride": a program that writes a single line of quiet, earned pride — a father who left school at fourteen for the docks, hearing his daughter read her college acceptance letter aloud — to `entry.txt`.

## The line

> Her father left school at fourteen for the docks; tonight his daughter read him her college acceptance letter aloud, and he asked her to read it again.

## About the line

A lifetime of sacrifice on one side of the semicolon, a college acceptance letter on the other — and the pride lands in the last five words, when he asks her to read it again. No trophy, no podium, no graduation stage: just a kitchen-table moment where a long effort finally lands. The line was chosen from eight candidates written for this entry, scored against the competition's own judge criteria (genuine earned pride, a concrete scene with a real person, pride earned inside the line, landing in one read, freshness), then revised once before finalizing. It is 151 characters, one line.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `entry.txt` beside `main.py`, or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set. Python 3, standard library only, deterministic — no network, no clock, no randomness.
